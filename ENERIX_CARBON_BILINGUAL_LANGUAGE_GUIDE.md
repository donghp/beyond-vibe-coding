# Governing Rules for Vietnamese-English Technical Copywriting

## 1. Core Language Philosophy
- **Vietnamese = Human-First**: All public-facing explanations, value propositions, and editorial narratives must be crafted in natural, professional Vietnamese suited for enterprise leaders and compliance officers in Vietnam.
- **English Technical Terminology = Canonical**: Industry-standard technical terms (`Scope 1`, `Scope 2`, `Scope 3`, `GHG Protocol`, `Activity Data`, `Emission Factor`, `CO2e`, `GWP`) retain their canonical English form in technical contexts.

---

## 2. Primary Sources & Reference Hierarchy
1. **Primary Bilingual Technical Reference**: `015.GHG_Protocol_SO-TAY-Phat-THAI-KHI-NHA-KINH.md` (and official Vietnamese legal decrees).
2. **Secondary Contemporary English Product Reference**: Plan A (`/measure-emissions`) for contemporary SaaS carbon product terminology style (without copying copy or brand claims).
3. **Semantic Standard**: EETS-001 (`Term + Namespace + Domain + Context + Version`).

---

## 3. Strict Rules for Translation & Copywriting
- Do not translate legal document numbers, decree titles, or statutory identifiers into informal phrasing.
- Distinguish **Verification** (`Thẩm định` / `Kiểm định` of historical inventory statements) from **Validation** (`Xác nhận` / `Thẩm định dự án` of ex-ante project claims).
- Avoid keyword stuffing, sensational marketing verbs (`supercharge`, `empower`), and low-quality machine translations.
