# Source and Citation Policy

## Source integrity

The series must distinguish:
- What a cited source explicitly states.
- What the author infers from one or more sources.
- What is an engineering recommendation.
- What remains uncertain or unverified.

Never imply that an illustrative scenario is a real company deployment. Never invent case studies, benchmark scores, adoption statistics, performance claims, or quotations.

## Citation practice

1. Cite original research for research contributions.
2. Cite official documentation for product-specific behavior.
3. Identify version and access date for changing technical guidance.
4. Prefer direct source links over secondary summaries.
5. Keep a claim-to-source evidence table in each episode brief.
6. If sources conflict, explain the disagreement and avoid false certainty.

## Benchmark reporting

Always disclose dataset, sample size, query construction, ground truth, systems, model versions, index parameters, retrieval settings, metric definitions, runtime environment, and measurement method. Include limitations and uncertainty. Do not report isolated numbers as universal conclusions.

## Copyright-safe writing

Summarize in original language. Keep direct quotations short and attributed. Do not reproduce substantial third-party text or diagrams. Check licenses before copying code, figures, datasets, or assets.
