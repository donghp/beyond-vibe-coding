# RAG-017 — Hallucinations, Source Freshness, and Citations

**Phase:** Evaluation and Production  
**Status:** Planned · Research brief  
**Author:** Dong Hong · Beyond Vibe Coding

## Research purpose

Analyze unsupported claims, conflicting sources, stale knowledge, citation mapping, and source-grounding verification.

## Learning objectives and research questions

1. How do retrieval and generation errors produce hallucinations?
2. How can freshness and validity be enforced?
3. How can citations be checked against source spans?

- Hallucination
- Faithfulness
- Source Freshness
- Citation Grounding
- Contradictory Evidence
- Evaluation
