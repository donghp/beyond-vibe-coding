## Learning outcome

Ask AI to make one small, bounded improvement and verify that the first vertical slice still works.

## Required practical evidence

- The original behavior before the change.
- The small, explicit request given to AI.
- The visible change after the request.
- A regression check for create, save, and same-profile reopen.

Do not ask AI to redesign or rebuild the entire application in this lesson.
