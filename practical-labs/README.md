Welcome to the hands-on learning companion for **BEYOND VIBE CODING** by Dong Hong.

These lessons are written for people who may never have programmed before. You will start with a familiar everyday need, use Google AI Studio (GAIS) to create a blank web app, give the AI clear project instructions, and learn to build one small result at a time.

Our first learning project is **BVC Task Management — A Simple Daily Work Manager**.

> **One lesson = one practical outcome.**  
> Follow the steps. Copy the prepared prompt. Look at your own GAIS result. Check it before moving on.

## How each lesson works

1. **What you will achieve** — one clear, practical result.
2. **Why it matters** — a short explanation in everyday language.
3. **Follow the steps** — exactly where to go, what to click, and what to enter, based on observed screens.
4. **Copy the complete prompt** — prepared with context, scope, limitations, and expected results.
5. **See the real result** — authentic GAIS screenshots or output, with a caption explaining what to look at.
6. **Check your work** — a simple checklist and clear completion conditions.
7. **Continue** — Previous, Next, and Lesson Index links.

For write-capable tasks, BVC uses the seven-step process:

**UNDERSTAND → INSPECT → PREPARE → AUTHORIZE → EXECUTE → VERIFY → ACCEPT**

The **Prompt Readiness Gate** must be READY before AI is asked to make authorized changes. If project context or scope is missing, AI must inspect and report first instead of guessing.

## Screenshot and evidence policy

Screenshots must come from the actual tool session. They are captured at meaningful checkpoints, not at every click.

Never use generated mockups as evidence of a real GAIS screen. Never expose passwords, API keys, tokens, private account details, customer data, or other secrets.

The lesson status must reflect the evidence actually available:

- **OBSERVED:** visible in an authentic screenshot or actual file.
- **VERIFIED:** a behavior or check was actually performed and its result recorded.
- **UNKNOWN / NOT VERIFIED:** not established by the available evidence.
- **DRAFT / EVIDENCE REQUIRED:** the lesson still needs practical review or a real checkpoint.

A visible Preview is not proof of persistence, security, production readiness, or deployment. A successful AI response is not proof that the requested behavior works.

## What you need to begin

For Phase A, you need a modern web browser, an internet connection, and access to Google AI Studio. You do not need to know Python, JavaScript, HTML, or TypeScript before you start. You do not need VS Code, GitHub, Localhost, a database, Supabase, or Authentication for the Beginner starting path.

Later phases introduce technical tools only when they become useful and their learning scope is ready.

## Relationship to the book

The GitHub Practical Labs let readers experience the value of building with AI and learning by doing. **BEYOND VIBE CODING** provides the broader method: turn ideas into clear requirements, guide AI through bounded steps, review changes, verify behavior, and keep the human in control.

**Build something useful. Learn one clear step at a time.**
