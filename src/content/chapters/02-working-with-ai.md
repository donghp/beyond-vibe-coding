## Re-centering Human Agency

 Generative models do not understand the commercial, ethical, or aesthetic context of your application. They are statistical engines trained on past patterns. 

Therefore, the human developer must act as the primary architect, defining strict requirements and boundaries before any code is generated.

> "AI is a brilliant assistant, but a terrible director."

## The Feedback Loop of Clarity

A successful AI interaction is a conversations of precise specifications:
1. **Specify Intent:** State exact visual and functional outcomes.
2. **Review Code:** Inspect every generated statement line-by-line.
3. **Assert Constraints:** Actively reject code that exceeds the requested scope or violates conventions.

## Setting Up Rules

By writing structural boundaries and maintaining clean project rules (such as an `AGENTS.md` file), you give the AI agent the context it needs to execute correctly without guessing.
