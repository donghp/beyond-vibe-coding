## The Great Expansion

As AI drives down the marginal cost of writing code to near zero, the volume of code created will increase exponentially. This does not make developers obsolete; it makes the role of the *thoughtful* developer more critical than ever.

The developer's primary job is shifting from **writing syntax** to **system curation, verification, and experience design**.

> "The developer of tomorrow is an editor, an architect, and a guardian of intent."

## Human Uniqueness

While AI can predict the most likely next word, it cannot:
- Understand the profound emotional context of design
- Build deep trust and strategic alignment with human readers
- Take ultimate responsibility for the stability and security of a system

## The Path Forward

Embrace the tools, but master the fundamentals. Study layout math, understand typography pairing, respect user privacy, and design clean, static-first systems that represent the pinnacle of digital craftsmanship.
