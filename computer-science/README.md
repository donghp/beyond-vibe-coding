## Research and Evidence Policy

- Distinguish source-derived facts, measured results, illustrative examples, hypotheses, and interpretation.
- Prefer foundational papers, official specifications, architecture manuals, textbooks, and primary technical documentation.
- Do not invent personal experience, production incidents, benchmark results, or citations.
- Analogies are teaching aids, not evidence.
- Experiments must state environment, method, variables, and limitations.
- Big O is not a wall-clock performance prediction; cache-miss counts do not directly determine total runtime.
- Use diagrams when they clarify relationships, not as decoration.
- This research track complements BVC's project-centric engineering framework. It does not claim to ship a Computer Science teaching platform, benchmark suite, or AI orchestration runtime.
