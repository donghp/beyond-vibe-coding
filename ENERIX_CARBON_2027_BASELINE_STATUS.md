# ENERIX Carbon 2027 Regulatory Baseline Status Report
# Target Production Year: 2027
# Status: GOVERNED & HARDENED

## 1. Baseline Summary
The ENERIX Carbon platform is fully configured for the 2027 regulatory operating environment in Vietnam. All statutory requirements under the Law on Environmental Protection 2020, Decree 06/2022/NĐ-CP, Decree 119/2025/NĐ-CP, Decree 83/2026/NĐ-CP, and Decision 42/2026/QĐ-TTg are integrated into the core regulatory architecture.

---

## 2. Key Baseline Parameters (2027)
- **Primary Statutory Mandates**: Facility-level GHG inventory mandatory for industrial facilities exceeding 3,000 tCO₂e (or energy threshold equivalent).
- **Grid Emission Factor Baseline**: MONRE combined margin grid emission factor (0.7234 tCO₂e/MWh).
- **Reporting Deadlines**: Annual inventory submission and third-party verification deadline aligned with statutory schedules (March 31 of following year).
- **Standard Integration**: TCVN ISO 14064-1:2025 organizational carbon accounting and TCVN ISO 14064-3 verification standards fully mapped.

---

## 3. Governance Status
- **Source Authority**: Governed by strict authority hierarchy (Legal Source > Technical Standard > Numerical Dataset > Engineering Interpretation > AI Suggestion).
- **Temporal Enforcement**: Operational rules validated against effective dates, with Decision 42/2026/QĐ-TTg properly bounded to its 2026-09-25 effective date.
- **Immutability & Safety**: Historical records protected; AI restricted to non-authoritative advisory roles.
