# ENERIX Carbon SEO Semantic Architecture & Information Architecture
# Version: 3.0.0
# Target Operation Year: 2027 Baseline

## 1. Overview & Objectives
The ENERIX Carbon SEO Semantic Architecture establishes a robust, search-optimized structure designed for organic discoverability across Vietnam's enterprise carbon accounting, GHG inventory, and decarbonization sectors. It strictly avoids keyword stuffing, doorway pages, and low-value AI content farms, focusing instead on semantic relevance, information gain, and authoritative source grounding.

---

## 2. Primary Public Information Architecture (IA)
- `/carbon/` — Home (Public Overview & Platform Core)
- `/carbon/solutions/` — Solutions (Enterprise Carbon Accounting & MRV)
- `/carbon/industries/` — Industries (Sector-Specific GHG Inventory & Compliance)
- `/carbon/science/` — Science (Calculation Methodologies, GHG Protocol, TCVN ISO 14064)
- `/carbon/resources/` — Resources (Regulatory Documents, Handbook, Guides)
- `/carbon/company/` — Company (About, Trust, Enterprise Security)

## 3. Topic Clusters & Entities
- **Core Topic**: Kiểm kê khí nhà kính (GHG Inventory)
